TIER LIST

Meta hero rankings

🏆 S-TIER (MUST HAVE)

Shadow

Top-tier assassin. Burst damage dominates.

Best for: PvP, Raids

Oracle

Essential healer. Keeps team alive.

Best for: All content

Archmage

Massive AOE. Clears waves fast.

Best for: PvE, Events

⚔️ A-TIER (SOLID CHOICES)

Ironclad

Reliable main tank. Great utility.

Best for: PvE content

Blademaster

Consistent single-target damage.

Best for: Boss fights

Cryomancer

Control + damage hybrid.

Best for: Arena PvP

💙 B-TIER (VIABLE)

Guardian

Good off-tank. Decent crowd control.

Best for: Tower Defense

Mystic

Utility support. Buffs team.

Best for: Raid support

⚪ C-TIER (NICHE USE)

Rookie Heroes

Early game only. Replace later.

Use for: Starter team only

📊 PvP vs PvE TIERS

PvP Meta

  • 1. Shadow - Burst assassin
  • 2. Cryomancer - Control mage
  • 3. Oracle - Essential heals
  • 4. Guardian - Defensive tank

PvE Meta

  • 1. Archmage - AOE damage
  • 2. Oracle - Healing priority
  • 3. Ironclad - Survivability
  • 4. Blademaster - Boss DPS
