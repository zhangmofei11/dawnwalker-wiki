About The Game

The Blood of Dawnwalker is an open-world dark fantasy action RPG developed by Rebel Wolves, a studio founded by former CD Projekt Red developers. You play as Coen, a man cursed to become a vampire at night while remaining human by day. With only 30 days to save your family, every choice matters in this non-linear, narrative-driven adventure set in 14th-century dark fantasy Europe.
