HEROES GUIDE

All heroes and their abilities

🛡️ TANK HEROES

Ironclad

  • • Ultimate: Shield Wall
  • • Passive: Damage Reduction
  • • Role: Main tank, team protection
  • • Best paired with: Healers

Guardian

  • • Ultimate: Divine Shield
  • • Passive: Taunt enemies
  • • Role: Off-tank, crowd control
  • • Best paired with: DPS heroes

⚔️ DPS HEROES

Blademaster

  • • Ultimate: Whirlwind Strike
  • • Passive: Critical Chance
  • • Role: Single target damage
  • • Best paired with: Tanks

Shadow

  • • Ultimate: Death Mark
  • • Passive: Backstab damage
  • • Role: Burst damage, assassin
  • • Best paired with: Crowd control

💚 SUPPORT HEROES

Oracle

  • • Ultimate: Mass Heal
  • • Passive: Healing boost
  • • Role: Primary healer
  • • Best paired with: All teams

Mystic

  • • Ultimate: Time Warp
  • • Passive: Cooldown reduction
  • • Role: Buffs, utility
  • • Best paired with: Heavy DPS

🔮 MAGE HEROES

Archmage

  • • Ultimate: Meteor Storm
  • • Passive: AOE damage boost
  • • Role: AOE damage dealer
  • • Best paired with: Control heroes

Cryomancer

  • • Ultimate: Frozen Orb
  • • Passive: Slow enemies
  • • Role: Crowd control, damage
  • • Best paired with: Melee DPS

👥 TEAM COMPOSITIONS

Balanced Team

  • • 1 Tank (Ironclad)
  • • 1 Support (Oracle)
  • • 1 Mage (Archmage)
  • • 1 DPS (Blademaster)

Aggressive Team

  • • 1 Off-tank (Guardian)
  • • 2 DPS (Shadow + Blademaster)
  • • 1 Support (Mystic)
